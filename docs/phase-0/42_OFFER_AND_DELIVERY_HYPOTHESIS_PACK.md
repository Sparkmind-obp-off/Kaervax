## Evidence Basis

This pack is derived from E-P0-001 through E-P0-006 in `41_DEMAND_DISCOVERY_EVIDENCE_PACK.md`.

**Observed:** public buyers requested bounded websites/landing pages with contact, booking, registration, WhatsApp, or payment actions; short turnaround and cost sensitivity were visible.

**Derived:** the repeated problem is not “absence of software” but the inability to turn a defined offer into a credible, usable conversion path with a clear handoff.

**Synthetic:** the KAERVAX package, wording, acceptance criteria, qualification questions, and operating workflow below are proposed constructs. They are not buyer-approved facts.

## Repeated Problem Pattern

- **Problem:** small business has a real service/product but lacks a coherent digital route from explanation to action.
- **Observed triggers:** launch, frustration with DIY setup, credibility problems, need for bookings/inquiries/payment, and time pressure.
- **Requested outcome:** professional page plus a simple action path.
- **Evidence supporting repetition:** E-P0-001, E-P0-002, E-P0-003, E-P0-004, E-P0-005, and E-P0-006.
- **Unresolved uncertainty:** Indonesian willingness to pay Rp1.500.000 for the proposed bundle; degree of demand for payment setup versus inquiry-only flow; acceptable maintenance model.

## Smallest Useful Deliverable

### Customer-visible outcome

One published, mobile-responsive conversion page for one existing product/service that:

1. explains the customer, problem, offer, benefits, and boundaries;
2. contains one primary CTA;
3. routes the CTA to one agreed action: WhatsApp inquiry, contact form, booking link, or authorized payment instruction/link;
4. includes basic FAQ/objection handling;
5. includes a simple handoff guide.

### Customer inputs required

- one product/service description and current price;
- target customer and service area;
- approved business/contact details;
- logo, images, and proof assets the customer owns or may lawfully use;
- desired primary CTA;
- existing domain/account access through an approved secure mechanism when needed;
- customer terms and fulfillment information.

### Delivery format

- one live conversion page or deployable page package;
- final approved copy;
- one configured CTA/action path;
- concise operating/handover instructions;
- delivery evidence reference and acceptance checklist.

### Delivery boundary

KAERVAX configures only one page, one offer, and one primary path. Third-party approval, buyer traffic, leads, sales, and revenue are outside KAERVAX's control.

### Acceptance condition

Delivery is accepted when the customer confirms or the verification checklist demonstrates that:

- agreed content is present;
- page works on current mobile and desktop browsers;
- primary CTA reaches the agreed destination;
- no critical defect blocks the core path;
- approved in-scope revision requests are resolved;
- handover information is delivered.

## Included

- review and structuring of one existing offer;
- copy for one conversion page;
- one responsive conversion page;
- one primary CTA/action path;
- basic FAQ and objection handling;
- basic deployment/configuration for the chosen surface;
- up to two reasonable in-scope revision rounds;
- handover instructions;
- 5–7 business-day target after all prerequisites are satisfied.

## Excluded

- guaranteed traffic, leads, conversion, sales, or revenue;
- advertising spend or campaign management;
- custom application, SaaS, CRM, ERP, mobile app, or multi-page portal;
- custom payment-gateway development;
- more than one product, funnel, page, or primary integration;
- complex booking, inventory, membership, account, or automation logic;
- logo creation, photography, or acquisition of licensed assets unless separately scoped;
- legal, tax, accounting, or regulated-industry compliance advice;
- unlimited revisions, support, integrations, or maintenance;
- provider fees, domain fees, or paid third-party services.

- customer has a legitimate product/service and can fulfill it;

- retain minimal evidence without unnecessary personal data.

## Executable Manual Delivery Workflow

```text
REQUEST
→ record source and buyer authority
→ QUALIFY
→ verify one offer, one audience, one action, deliverability, timing, and fit
→ CONFIRM SCOPE
→ written inclusions, exclusions, dependencies, price, timeline, revisions, acceptance
→ PAYMENT
→ issue authorized payment instruction; start only after authoritative verification
→ PREPARE
→ collect inputs securely; draft copy; build/configure page and action path
→ INTERNAL VERIFY
→ mobile/desktop, links, CTA destination, content, privacy, ownership
→ DELIVER
→ send approved delivery reference and handover
→ ACCEPT / REVISE
→ record explicit acceptance or up to two in-scope revision rounds
→ RECORD OUTCOME
→ response, payment, effort, delivery time, objections, acceptance, issues, learning
```

## Payment Path and Authority Boundary

- **Commercial hypothesis:** Rp1.500.000, 100% upfront.
- **Initial method:** an invoice/payment instruction issued by the authorized KAERVAX operator. Duitku may be used only if an authorized merchant configuration exists; otherwise an approved business bank-transfer path may be documented for the trial.
- **Authoritative payment evidence:** verified provider/bank confirmation matched to the offer and payer reference.
- **Not sufficient:** checkout opened, redirect completed, screenshot alone, verbal claim, or sandbox transaction.
- **Authority:** only the human business operator may approve the offer, payment account, refund terms, discounts, and financial action. This execution session does not charge or refund anyone.
- **Delivery gate:** work begins only after payment verification and receipt of required inputs, unless the operator explicitly authorizes a different documented experiment.
