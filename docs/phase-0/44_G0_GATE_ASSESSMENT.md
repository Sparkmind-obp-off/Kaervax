## Evidence Summary

### Observed

- Six public demand records were retrieved from two public source types.
- At least three records explicitly request a simple business page; several add contact, booking, registration, WhatsApp, or payment actions.
- Indonesian comparable budgets observed are Rp500.000–Rp1.000.000.
- The closest 2026 Indonesian request included a five-day deadline and Rp600.000–Rp850.000 budget.
- No KAERVAX offer was sent, no buyer response was received, and no payment or delivery occurred.

### Derived

- A bounded “one offer → one conversion page → one action path” service is directionally aligned with observed requests.
- The current Rp1.500.000 hypothesis may face price friction in the observed Indonesian marketplace segment.
- Qualification must reduce bundled website/email/payment requests to fixed scope or reject/quote separately.

### Synthetic

- OFFER-P0-V1 wording and the candidate-specific draft message.
- Candidate scores and expected objections.
- Any projected conversion, delivery outcome, or willingness to pay.

## Blockers

1. No real buyer interaction has been executed.
2. No exact candidate/channel communication has specific human approval and verified account access.
3. Buyer identity/payment authority has not been confirmed.
4. The payment account, refund terms, and authoritative verification mechanism for the first trial are not confirmed.
5. The closest Indonesian candidate's budget is below the V1 price and its current availability/status has not been verified.

## Risk of False Advancement

Moving to Phase 1 as though G0 passed would convert public market observations and an internal offer draft into a false claim of direct validation. A deployment or a green technical test does not resolve requirement G.

## Required Evidence to Unblock G0

Minimum next evidence:

1. one current, legitimate candidate with verified relevance and contactability;
2. operator approval for exact offer version, recipient, and channel;
3. proof the offer was sent/received or otherwise directly presented;
4. real buyer response, including objection, acceptance, or rejection;
5. confirmation of the authorized payment path and commercial terms;
6. updated operating record at the strongest verified state.

A paid transaction is valuable but is not required by this G0 text; however, no acceptance or payment may be inferred without direct evidence.

## G0 Decision

**PARTIAL / BLOCKED**

Phase 0 produced directionally useful demand and offer evidence, but G0 does not pass because real buyer interaction/direct validation and payment authority readiness are incomplete.

**Phase state:** remain in Phase 0.  
**Phase 1:** not authorized by this assessment.  
**Next decision:** operator selects and approves one real candidate/channel/payment path for the next operating trial.
