# Kaerfak Offer & Revenue Architecture

## 1. Offer Ladder
```
ENTRY
↓
PRODUCT
↓
IMPLEMENTATION
↓
BUSINESS SYSTEM
↓
CUSTOM / HIGH-TIER
↓
ONGOING SUPPORT
```

## 2. Entry
Low-friction discovery, audit, consultation, or small product where appropriate.

## 3. Product
Repeatable systems with defined scope and delivery.

## 4. Implementation
Configuration, integration, migration, automation, or deployment.

## 5. Custom / High-Tier
Purpose-built systems for customers with unique workflows. Pricing is scope-based and should reflect complexity, responsibility, delivery effort, and value.

## 6. Recurring
Maintenance, hosting management, support, monitoring, iteration, or other genuinely recurring value may become subscription/retainer revenue.

## 7. Pricing Rule
Do not publish artificial prices merely to make the brand look established. Prices should be tied to an actual offer and delivery model.
