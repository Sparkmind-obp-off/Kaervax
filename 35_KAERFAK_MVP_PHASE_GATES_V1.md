# Kaerfak MVP & Phase Gates V1

## Phase 0 — Business Foundation
Exit when:
- positioning is clear,
- customer profile is defined,
- offer ladder exists,
- transaction loop is documented.

## Phase 1 — Sell Before Scale
Exit when:
- at least one concrete offer is market-ready,
- outreach/discovery is active,
- customer objections are recorded,
- first transaction attempt is underway.

## Phase 2 — Deliver
Exit when:
- a paid customer receives a working outcome,
- delivery process is documented,
- evidence/case material exists.

## Phase 3 — Productize
Exit when:
- repeated demand reveals common requirements,
- reusable product boundaries are clear,
- Runner OS or another product has a validated customer segment.

## Phase 4 — Systemize
Only after evidence:
- automate operations,
- improve onboarding,
- add recurring support,
- expand product catalog.

## Gate Rule
No major engineering expansion without evidence that the previous commercial gate has been passed.
