# Kaerfak Business Model V1

## 1. Customer
Initial customer profile:
- UMKM and small businesses
- organizations/institutions
- professionals/creators with operational workflows
- customers needing a tailored digital system

## 2. Problems
Typical problems:
- fragmented manual workflows,
- weak digital presence,
- repetitive administration,
- disconnected tools,
- lack of visibility,
- no purpose-built internal system.

## 3. Value Proposition
Kaerfak designs and delivers practical digital systems that connect a customer's real workflow to a usable interface, automation, integration, or operational dashboard.

## 4. Commercial Model
Kaerfak can monetize through:
- fixed-scope products,
- implementation services,
- setup/integration services,
- custom systems,
- maintenance or recurring support where justified.

## 5. Business Loop
Demand → qualification → offer → payment → delivery → evidence → repeat/referral → reinvestment.

## 6. Validation Rule
A concept is not considered commercially validated merely because it is built. Validation requires external demand and preferably a real payment or other strong buying signal.

## 7. V1 Constraint
Start with a narrow offer that can be sold and delivered manually before adding complex platform infrastructure.
