# KAERVAX — Business Architecture

**Status:** FOUNDATION / REVENUE-FIRST

## 1. Business Model
KAERVAX is designed as a hybrid operator business:

1. **Services** generate near-term cash flow and provide direct market evidence.
2. **Products** package repeatable solutions once demand is proven.
3. **Data / intelligence assets** compound learning from observed demand and outcomes.
4. **Software** automates repeatable portions of the proven workflow.

This order is intentional: demand first, build second.

## 2. Core Business Loop
```text
DEMAND DISCOVERY
      ↓
DEMAND INTELLIGENCE
      ↓
OPPORTUNITY SELECTION
      ↓
OFFER CREATION
      ↓
CONTENT / DISTRIBUTION
      ↓
TRAFFIC / CONVERSATION
      ↓
TRANSACTION
      ↓
DELIVERY / EXECUTION
      ↓
OUTCOME + FEEDBACK
      ↓
LEARNING
      └──────────────→ DEMAND DISCOVERY
```

## 3. Revenue Layers
### Layer A — Done-for-you
Solve a customer's concrete problem manually with AI-assisted delivery.

### Layer B — Productized service
Turn repeated work into a defined package with clear inputs, outputs, timeline, and price.

### Layer C — Workflow/product
Automate stable parts of the delivery process.

### Layer D — Intelligence/data
Package useful aggregated signals, benchmarks, opportunity feeds, or decision support where legally and commercially appropriate.

### Layer E — Platform
Only build broader SaaS/platform infrastructure after repeated demand and workflow evidence.

## 4. Customer Acquisition Logic
KAERVAX should not depend on one channel. Initial discovery can monitor:
- Threads
- X
- Instagram
- TikTok
- Facebook
- Google/Search
- marketplaces
- WhatsApp / Telegram communities where access is permitted

The acquisition layer should record **observable demand**, not merely engagement metrics.

## 5. Opportunity Record
Every meaningful opportunity should be representable as structured data:

- source
- captured_at
- original demand text / reference
- problem category
- buyer/persona
- urgency
- stated or inferred budget signal
- requested outcome
- delivery complexity
- confidence
- evidence links
- proposed offer
- status
- response
- transaction outcome
- delivery outcome
- learning

## 6. Scoring Framework
The first version should score opportunities on transparent dimensions rather than an opaque AI number:

`Opportunity Score = Demand Evidence + Buyer Clarity + Urgency + Commercial Fit + Delivery Feasibility + Repeatability`

Scores are decision-support signals, not truth. Human review remains available.

## 7. Business Operating Rules
1. No major feature is justified only because it sounds useful.
2. A feature must support demand discovery, selling, delivery, learning, or operational control.
3. Real customer evidence outranks internal assumptions.
4. Payment is stronger validation than clicks.
5. Delivery evidence outranks prototype completeness.
6. External AI/tools are workers; the business logic and important records remain owned by KAERVAX.
7. Provider dependencies must be replaceable where feasible.

## 8. First Commercial Experiment
The first KAERVAX experiment should target one narrow, reachable problem and seek a **real paid transaction before building the full platform**.

Minimum loop:
`find demand → qualify → create offer → distribute/contact → collect payment → deliver → record outcome`

The software MVP exists to reduce friction in this loop, not to simulate a large company.

## 9. North-Star Operating Metrics
Track:
- qualified demand captured
- opportunities accepted
- offers sent
- conversations started
- paid transactions
- revenue
- delivery time
- gross margin estimate
- repeat/referral signals
- learning entries

Avoid vanity-first metrics such as total AI calls or number of screens.

## 10. Expansion Gate
A new vertical, product, or automation layer should pass:

**Evidence → Repeatability → Economics → Operational feasibility → Build.**

If evidence is weak, return to discovery instead of expanding the system.
