# Kaerfak Transaction Architecture

## Core Loop
```
Demand
  ↓
Lead
  ↓
Qualification
  ↓
Offer
  ↓
Agreement
  ↓
Payment
  ↓
Delivery
  ↓
Acceptance
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Repeat / Referral / Support
```

## Transaction Records
At minimum track:
- customer,
- problem,
- offer,
- quoted amount,
- payment status,
- delivery status,
- outcome,
- follow-up opportunity.

## Revenue Integrity
Revenue is counted from actual completed commercial transactions, not hypothetical value.

## Operating Rule
The system should make it easy to see what is sold, what is paid, what is delivered, and what remains outstanding.
