# Kaerfak Customer Architecture

## 1. Customer Hierarchy
### Primary
Customers with a concrete operational problem and a clear willingness or ability to pay.

### Secondary
Customers exploring a solution but not yet ready to purchase.

### Discovery
People who can reveal recurring problems, language, budgets, workflows, and unmet demand.

## 2. Qualification Signals
Capture:
- problem,
- urgency,
- current workaround,
- desired outcome,
- budget signal,
- decision maker,
- deadline,
- technical constraints.

## 3. Customer Journey
Discover → Understand → Qualify → Recommend → Scope → Quote → Pay → Build → Deliver → Support → Expand.

## 4. Evidence
Every stage should create evidence that improves the next commercial decision.

## 5. Principle
Kaerfak serves the customer's problem first. Technology is selected after the problem and commercial scope are understood.
