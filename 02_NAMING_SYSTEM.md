# KAERVAX — Naming System

**Status:** FINAL / LOCKED

## 1. Master Brand
**KAERVAX** is the locked master brand.

Naming discovery is closed. Do not generate replacement master-brand candidates unless a future explicit rebrand decision is made.

## 2. Naming Architecture
Use this hierarchy:

`KAERVAX → Domain / Capability → Product / Offer → Feature / Component`

Examples:
- KAERVAX Core
- KAERVAX Demand Intelligence
- KAERVAX Opportunity Engine
- KAERVAX Services
- KAERVAX [descriptive product name]

## 3. Naming Principles
- Master brand remains KAERVAX.
- Prefer descriptive capability names for internal modules.
- Product names should communicate a distinct job, not merely sound technical.
- Avoid unnecessary acronyms.
- Avoid creating a separate brand for every feature.
- Names must remain usable in Indonesian and international contexts.
- Technical component names should be treated as implementation identifiers unless they earn public product status.

## 4. Naming Decision Gate
A new public product name requires all of:
1. distinct customer problem;
2. distinct offer or value proposition;
3. repeatable delivery model;
4. evidence of demand;
5. reason to separate the product identity from KAERVAX.

Without these conditions, keep the name descriptive under KAERVAX.

## 6. Forbidden Naming Drift
Do not casually rename the same concept across documentation. In particular, do not alternate between multiple names for the core engine merely because a new term sounds better.

## 7. Lock
**KAERVAX = FINAL NAME LOCK.** The next work is architecture and execution, not another naming exploration cycle.
