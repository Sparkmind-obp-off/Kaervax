## 1. Product Role

Kaerfak is the commercial business. Products sit beneath Kaerfak and must earn independent product identity through evidence.

## 2. Product Families

```
KAERFAK
├── Products
│   └── Runner OS
├── Services
│   ├── Automation
│   ├── Integration
│   └── Digital Systems
└── Custom Systems
    └── High-Tier / Private Instances
```

## 3. Runner OS

Runner OS is a Kaerfak product/system for running, training, event preparation, progress, planning, and related personal workflows.

It may have:
- a reusable productized version;
- configurable customer instances;
- isolated private instances.

Runner OS is not the parent business and is not required to be the primary commercial offer.

## 4. Productization Rule

A capability becomes a public product only when:
1. a distinct customer problem is demonstrated;
2. the value proposition is clear;
3. delivery can be repeated;
4. demand evidence exists;
5. separation from the Kaerfak master brand is commercially useful.

Until then, keep it as a Kaerfak capability, service, experiment, or custom system.

## 5. Build Rule

Do not build a product merely because it can be built. Product scope must be justified by observed demand, delivery evidence, repeatability, or a concrete commercial experiment.
